The short version

Be kind to staff and fellow guests, and the rooftop stays lovely for everyone. Prices on the menu board rule at the counter; online trays are a convenience, not a contract.

Orders and payment

Dine-in and takeaway only — we do not deliver. Payment happens at the counter. GST applies as shown at checkout. If a dish runs out, we will suggest the closest alternative or take it off your bill.

Reservations

Tables are held for 15 minutes past the agreed time on busy evenings. Groups above six should book a day ahead on 092740 73791.

The website

Menu, hours and photos are kept fresh but the board at the cafe wins on the day. Do not misuse the forms — spam and abuse get blocked.
