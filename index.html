Good to know

Questions,
answered.

Anything else? Call 092740 73791, we reply fast.

What are your hours?

Open every day, till 12 midnight. Rooftop seats fill fast after sunset, so come early for golden hour.

Do you deliver?

No delivery. Everything is dine-in or takeaway, so it reaches you exactly as the kitchen intended.

Can I reserve a rooftop table?

Yes. Call 092740 73791 and tell us the evening and headcount. Groups above six should book a day ahead.

What should I order first?

Pistachio Latte, Cold Brew Tonic, Pink Spaghetti, and a house mocktail. Those four built our 4.6 rating.

Where exactly are you?

VIP Road, opposite Marina Grand Hospital, Vesu, Surat 395007. Plus code 4QP6+66. Street parking outside.
